Love Ngo

Love, Ngo – is a story of Ngongo a person with Speech impediment or nasal speech defect a physical condition where it affects their face and way of speaking. he and his friends who are also people with similar conditions volunteers at a cleft lip foundation and works at his mother’s burger stand where He falls for a woman, but her family rejects him due to his condition and social status, leading him to face challenges with love and acceptance with the help of his friends.

A Filipino Comedy Movie Starring Malupiton, Jared Napoles , Candy Pangilingan and Gina alajar.

Plot

The story follows characters who experience unexpected situations involving love, friendship, and everyday life. As they deal with different challenges and misunderstandings, they learn more about relationships and what it means to care for someone.

Cast

Malupiton plays one of the main roles in the movie. His comedic personality brings humor to the story and makes many of the scenes entertaining. The other characters help develop the story through their interactions and experiences.

Reviews

For me, Love, Ngo is an enjoyable movie because it combines comedy with a simple story about love and relationships. I like how Malupiton's humor makes the movie more fun and relatable. Overall, it is a light and entertaining movie that can be enjoyed with friends or family.
